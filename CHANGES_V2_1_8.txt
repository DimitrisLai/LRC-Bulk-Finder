LRC Bulk Finder v2.1.8 — Sidecar Candidate Management
Created by Dimitris L.

1. New startup menu: [1] SEARCH, [2] PROMOTE CANDIDATES.
2. Unverified timed review candidate written as Song.candidate.lrc
   BESIDE Song.mp3; more choices use Song.candidate.2.lrc, etc.
3. If Song.candidate.lrc already exists, no overwrite. Identical
   content reused; different content given next numbered candidate.
4. Promotion mode searches selected folder and (optionally) subfolders,
   previews matching files, asks for one confirmation, then promotes only
   Song.candidate.lrc to Song.lrc if Song.mp3 exists and Song.lrc does not.
5. Never edits MP3 audio, never overwrites approved .lrc, and never
   automatically promotes second/third alternate candidates.
6. Saves a promotion CSV with per-file success and reason for skips.
7. Keeps v2.1.7 LRCLIB priority/recovery and timeout-only retries.
8. Legacy random-named .candidate.txt files are NOT automatically mapped.
9. No PDF in this interim build.
