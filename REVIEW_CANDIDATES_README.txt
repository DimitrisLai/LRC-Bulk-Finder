REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATES - v2.1.4
Created by Dimitris L.

Potential synchronized lyrics without confirmed matching artist/title,
recording version, or duration are NOT automatically installed.
Look in LRC_Finder_Review_v2_1_4.csv for source, rejection reason and
candidate .txt path. The MP3's correct .lrc sidecar is never overwritten.

NEW: LRC_Review_Needed.txt lists tracks with candidate(s) requiring a
human decision. LRC_Not_Found.txt now lists only completed searches
without accepted lyrics OR review candidates; a source may be unavailable.

Manually verify both song identity and timing against the recording
before creating a .lrc from a reviewed candidate.
