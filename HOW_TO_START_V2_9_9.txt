LRC Bulk Finder v2.9.9 — Candidate Review progress and alternatives deletion

1. Extract the complete standalone ZIP into a new folder. Keep v2.9.8 as backup.
2. Choose your music folder and open Candidate Review > Refresh candidates.
3. Under batch actions, observe the progress bar and N / total (percent) label.
4. Promote selected primaries or all eligible primaries: the progress bar tracks file-by-file results. Promotions stay MARKED UNVERIFIED.
5. For deletion, select numbered alternative rows or click Select visible alternatives.
6. Click Delete selected alternatives (Recycle Bin) and confirm the count and filename preview. Only selected numbered .candidate.N.lrc files are recycled.
7. Primary candidate files, installed .lrc, audio and ID3 are never recycled. Failure to access Recycle Bin never triggers permanent deletion.
8. Stop batch ends between files and leaves a partial progress count; the normal search is not allowed during batch work.
9. Recycle Bin may allow you to restore discarded alternatives. Deleting an alternative does not automatically renumber surviving alternatives.
10. Test first with copies of a few music and alternative candidate files.
