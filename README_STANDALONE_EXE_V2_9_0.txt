LRC BULK FINDER v2.9.0 — SMART LIBRARY & STABILITY

1. Extract the ENTIRE Windows standalone ZIP; keep both EXEs together.
2. Run LRC_Control_Center.exe. Choose English or Ελληνικά.
3. Select the music folder. Open Sync Lab (experimental) > Smart Library.
4. Scan / incremental refresh. To check duration alignment, optionally select Deep duration checks (slower).
5. Browse 200 results per page, filter/search and mark problematic rows Bookmarked or Reviewed.
6. Double-click selected audio to open read-only inputs in Lyrics Studio. Save only a NEW UNVERIFIED candidate, never an original.
7. Cancel a scan safely; unfinished SQLite updates rollback, and your prior successful index remains.
8. Export a filtered CSV ONLY into a new filename (existing reports are never overwritten).
9. Full karaoke and instrumentals remain under the Karaoke Edition nested workspace.
10. For your planned one-day pre-v3.0 test, follow TEST_AUDIT_V2_9_0.md and BOTH English/Greek PDF manuals.
11. Smart Library metadata cache: %APPDATA%\LRCBulkFinder\smart_library_v290.sqlite3. No raw music or complete lyrics stored in that cache.
12. Windows binaries are unsigned until separate independent SignPath signing is approved. Verify official release SHA-256.

No automatic vocal removal or word-level synchronization. Test using a COPY of your music folder.
