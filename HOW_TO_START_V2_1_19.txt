LRC BULK FINDER v2.1.19 - ARTIST NAMES / WINDOWS TEST QUICK START
Created by Dimitris L. - PUBLIC BETA

WHAT CHANGED:
  NEW Greek_Artists.csv: 113 standalone Greek artist/group name mappings.
  Artist names are looked up as COMPLETE credits, not word-by-word.
  Approved original-to-Greek artist variants take priority over uncertain AI.
  UNKNOWN artist names remain in Latin letters; the AI will not invent names.
  Known collaborations can use recognized artist names, keeping separators.
  English-first mixed Greek titles are preserved, e.g. Sexy koritsia
  searches also as Sexy κορίτσια - NOT Σεξυ κορίτσια.

INSTALL A COMPLETE VERSION:
1. Download the v2.1.19 WINDOWS RELEASE ZIP, not GitHub's Source code.zip.
2. Extract the WHOLE ZIP into a NEW folder. Keep your old installation.
3. Copy ONLY your own edited Greek_Aliases.csv / Greek_Words.csv from the
   previous installation as desired. Personal aliases override public ones.
4. If using your huge LRCLIB SQLite, reconnect it through the launcher
   CONNECT_EXISTING_LRCLIB_DATABASE.cmd or copy LRCLIB_LOCAL_DB.txt.
5. Double-click START_HERE.cmd (Python 3.10+ and required packages).

COMPLETE OPTION 1 - ACCURACY-FIRST TEST PROMPTS, IN ORDER:
1) Choose [1/2/3/4] (default 1):                     1
2) Folder chooser / paste MP3 folder path:             [your 20-track test folder]
3) Greeklish / Greek folder? [y/N]:                    Y
4) Use your installed LOCAL G2G AI model? [y/N]:       Y (only if installed)
5) MusicBrainz extra artist/title metadata? [Y/n]:    Y
6) Search mode FAST or FULL? [F/full]:                 full
7) Wait for temporary provider recovery? [Y/n]:       N (avoid hours of waiting)
8) Include subfolders? [Y/n]:                          Y
9) Skip completed NO_MATCH from last 7 days? [Y/n]:   N (fresh controlled test)
10) How many missing songs? [20 for test, A for all]:  20
11) LRCLIB exact-signature fallback? [y/N]:           Y
12) Auto-search after Greek/AI preview? [Y/n]:         N FIRST to review spellings;
                                                     Y for second full test.

IMPORTANT: Artist names must be accurate. This build never uses speculative
AI artist spellings as alternatives. The original ID3 artist and title are
ALWAYS the first query; then original title plus official Greek artist;
then approved Greek title plus official Greek artist when available.

Other modes:
2 = Promote candidate sidecar to .lrc with [00:00.00] candidate marker.
3 = LRCLIB-only catch-up for queued incomplete searches (no MusicBrainz).
4 = LOCAL LRCLIB SQLite only, no HTTP / MusicBrainz; separate from
    the small Greek_Lexicon.sqlite3 vocabulary cache.

PUBLIC FILES contain a generic Greek word and artist vocabulary,
NOT personal filenames, folder paths, or the 5,686-track inventory.
Both public dictionaries are editable CSVs; your private
Greek_Aliases.csv has final authority.
