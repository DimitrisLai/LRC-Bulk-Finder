LRC BULK FINDER v2.4.2 — ONE-CLICK STOP AND FIVE COUNTERS
PUBLIC BETA. Complete Python/CMD Windows ZIP or optional standalone GUI.

OPEN_CONTROL_CENTER.cmd — Control Center with Greek/Night/offline presets.
START_HERE.cmd — original classic 5-mode command-line search.

STOP SEARCH — KEEP APP OPEN:
  Click once and confirm. The running SEARCH WORKER TREE is killed
  immediately, including its standalone EXE child processes.
  The Control Center remains open and can run another search.
  Previously completed song/stage checkpoints are durable;
  the currently unfinished song and unwritten final reports may be lost.

LIVE COUNTERS:
  Current stage (reset after each stage): Songs/LRC found,
  Songs/Candidate found (each song counts ONCE even with 2–3
  review variants), Not found (only definite misses).
  All stages: LRC Count and Candidate Count, both cumulative across
  all three stages and never reset until another new search.
