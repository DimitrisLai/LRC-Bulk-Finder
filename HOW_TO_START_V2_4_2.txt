STOP BUTTON (CHANGED from v2.4.1)
  1. Open LIVE PROGRESS, click STOP SEARCH - keep app open.
  2. Confirm immediate termination of ONLY the search worker process.
  3. Windows: stops the worker PROCESS TREE, including any packaged
     PyInstaller child processes. The Control Center remains running.
  4. No second click, console Ctrl+Break or 8-second wait is needed.
  5. Previously completed-song JSONL checkpoints are durable and remain.
     A currently running song and reports not yet written can be lost.
     Restart the saved profile to retry unfinished work.
  6. The GUI keeps previously observed totals after you press Stop;
     a NEW run resets all five counters to zero and then replays
     completed checkpoints as appropriate.
  7. Do not run two searches simultaneously against the same music folder.

FIVE LIVE COUNTERS, ARRANGED IN TWO GROUPS:
  THIS STAGE (first three; reset after EACH completed round):
    Songs / LRC found        - unique songs saved in current stage.
    Songs / Candidate found  - unique songs with ACTUAL candidate sidecars
                                in current stage (even 2-3 candidates = 1).
    Not found                - definite misses this stage, excluding timeouts,
                                transport errors and blocked providers.
  ALL STAGES (last two; NOT reset between stages):
    LRC Count                - unique songs for which verified LRC was saved.
    Candidate Count          - unique songs with a saved candidate review file.

For example: Stage 1 finds 10 LRCs and candidates on 5 different songs.
After Stage 1 finishes, the first three counters reset to 0 while the
totals remain LRC Count 10, Candidate Count 5. Stage 2 adds its NEW
findings to those totals without counting the same song twice in a category.
A candidate-bearing song saved with verified LRC later can appear in BOTH
historical totals. Previously installed LRCs skipped at the beginning of
a new run are NOT counted as newly found in that run.

No original MP3/FLAC/M4A/MP4/OGG/OPUS/WAV files or audio tags are modified.
Approved existing LRCs and candidate safety rules remain protected.
